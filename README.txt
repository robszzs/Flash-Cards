FOR MY PERSONAL USE

FLASHCARDS - how to use
1. Unzip this folder.
2. Double-click index.html (opens in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari). No install or internet needed.
3. Type a question and answer, click "Add card". Repeat for all your cards.
4. Choose "Keep in order" or "Shuffle", then click "Review".
5. Click the card (or press Space) to flip it. Use Next/Previous or the arrow keys.
Your cards are saved automatically in your browser, so they're still there next time.

MOVING CARDS TO ANOTHER DEVICE
- On the device that has your cards: click "Export". A file called flashcards.json downloads.
- Send that file to your other device (email, Messenger, Google Drive, etc.).
- On the other device: open the site, click "Import", and choose flashcards.json.
- Import adds the cards and skips any you already have, so it's safe to repeat.
